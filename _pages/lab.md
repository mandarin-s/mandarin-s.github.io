## The Gap Between "Feel" and Physics

I am a Materials Science student and competitive tennis player obsessed with one question: *What is actually happening when ball meets string?*

In engineering school, we are taught to determine a heuristically sufficient answer. In tennis, players claim to feel the nuance of a micron (though sometimes they are just looking for excuses). My goal is to bridge this gap. I treat the tennis racket not just as a piece of equipment, but as a complex system of viscoelastic polymers and composite laminates that must manage energy transfer (hysteresis) and vibration damping (entropy).

### My Core Mission:
I seek the **physical explanations for phenomenological experiences.** Do the intangible sensations players describe (such as "pocketing," "crispness," and "plushness") hold up to scientific scrutiny? Are these experiences epistemologically true, rooted in material reality, or are they merely cognitive biases born from marketing and hearsay?
