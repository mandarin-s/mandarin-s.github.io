## The Gap Between "Feel" and Physics

I am a Materials Science student and competitive tennis player obsessed with one question: *What is actually happening when ball meets string?*

In engineering school, we are taught to approximate. In tennis, players feel the nuance of a micron. My goal is to bridge that gap. I treat the tennis racket not just as a piece of equipment, but as a complex system of viscoelastic polymers and composite laminates that must manage energy transfer (hysteresis) and vibration damping (entropy).

### My Methodology

This site is my "Forensic Engineering" lab notebook. I don't rely on marketing brochures. I rely on:

1.  **Microscopy:** Analyzing the tribological wear of polyester strings.
2.  **Hardness Testing:** Mapping the Shore A vs. Shore D gradients in modern frames.
3.  **Beam Theory:** Calculating the true stiffness ($EI$) of racket geometries.

### Current Projects
I am currently working on a **Shore Hardness Mapping** of the Wilson Blade v9, analyzing how the handle foam density correlates with player comfort.
